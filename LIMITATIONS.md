# What is not proved here

Lean proves exactly the statements written, under exactly the hypotheses written.

**Proved (§2 of the paper, programme item T1).** The finite linear predictive quotient:
predictive equivalence, invariance and descent of the dynamics, finite closure by
Cayley–Hamilton, the rank formula, minimality among linear sufficient summaries, and the
closure criterion for the present observation. The setting is a finite-dimensional vector
space over an arbitrary field with linear dynamics and a linear observation.

**Not proved here.**

* Nonlinear or infinite-dimensional dynamics, and any stochastic or density-operator version
  of the quotient (the paper's §10 form).
* The remaining programme items T2–T10: star-projection tangent geometry and the
  metric–curvature inequality, the path-ledger (spectral-flow) descent theorem, the common-clock
  criterion, the finite observable-return criterion, the torus-knot peripheral and silence laws,
  the friction–metric comparison, the APS / Maslov calculations and the Maxwell–Dirac gate.
* Every physical hypothesis in the paper: the electron-memory hypothesis, the Gigantefermion
  hypothesis, path inheritance for light, the arrow architecture, and any gravitational or
  cosmological reading.

Formalization is evidence for the mathematics, not for the physical interpretation.
